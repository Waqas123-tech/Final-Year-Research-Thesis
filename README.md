Mapping the Landscape of Artificial Intelligence-Based Autism Spectrum Disorder Detection A Systematic Review of Multimodal Gains, Explainable AI Adoption, and Fairness Gaps

Author

Waqas Ahmad
BSc Computer Science Graduate (2026)
Department of Computer Science, Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan, Pakistan

Co-authors: Ashraf Zia and Muhammad Zakarya

Abstract

Although artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being applied to the automated detection of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), existing research has largely emphasized classification accuracy, with comparatively less attention to explainability and algorithmic fairness.

This systematic review, conducted in accordance with PRISMA criteria, examines AI-based ASD detection across three dimensions: explainable AI (XAI), multimodal data fusion, and fairness across demographic groups. A comprehensive search of major academic databases—including PubMed, IEEE Xplore, Scopus, and the ACM Digital Library identified 10,117 records, of which 45 primary studies met the inclusion criteria.

The findings indicate that the benefits of multimodal fusion depend on modality complementarity, baseline performance, and cross-site variability. Although 71.1% of the included studies reported some form of interpretability, only 31.1% employed rigorous post-hoc XAI techniques. Furthermore, only 13% conducted formal fairness audits. These findings highlight persistent gaps in patient-level explainability, demographic equity, and the clinical translation of AI-based ASD detection.

The review proposes a human-centered framework that jointly considers predictive performance, explainability, and fairness to support more clinically trustworthy and equitable AI systems.

Research Objectives

Examine the effectiveness of multimodal data fusion compared with single-modality approaches in ASD detection.

Assess the adoption of explainability methods in AI-based ASD research.

Investigate the extent to which existing studies evaluate algorithmic fairness and demographic disparities.

Identify methodological and translational gaps that limit the clinical reliability of AI-based ASD detection.

Propose a framework for more trustworthy, interpretable, and equitable healthcare AI.

Keywords

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), Artificial Intelligence, Explainable AI (XAI), Algorithmic Fairness, Multimodal Learning, Systematic Review, Clinical Translation, fMRI, Diagnostic Bias.
